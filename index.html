Free and open source · Windows 11 · Installer and portable executable

01 / CAPTURE

Your screen. Your selection.

Capture a region, a window, a monitor, or your whole desktop. Built with mixed-DPI, multi-monitor setups in mind.

02 / ANNOTATE

Make your point.

Draw arrows and shapes, highlight details, add text, and number your steps. Edit right on the captured image.

03 / KEEP GOING

Ready for the next step.

Copy to your clipboard, save to a file, or pin a capture on screen. Use the command line when you want to automate.

One key to get started.

Run greenflame, then press Print Screen. Drag to select a region, choose an annotation tool, and make it yours. The user guide covers the shortcuts and everything else.
